中文

本隐私政策说明 VeryGoodbye Access（以下简称“本应用”）在您使用 Google 登录时如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。本应用是 VeryGoodbye 个人自托管服务的登录入口，由 Cloudflare Access 提供访问保护。

1. 我们收集的信息

通过 Google 登录，本应用仅请求 openid、email 和 profile 权限范围，仅获取您的电子邮件地址以及姓名等基本个人资料信息。

2. 信息的用途

上述信息仅用于验证您的身份，并通过 Cloudflare Access 判断您是否被授权访问相关服务。只有所有者授权的账号可以访问。

3. 不访问其他 Google 用户数据

本应用不访问、不请求您的任何其他 Google 用户数据（例如 Gmail、云端硬盘、通讯录、日历等）。

4. 信息共享

我们不会出售、出租或与任何第三方共享您的信息，唯一例外是作为访问控制服务提供商的 Cloudflare（Cloudflare Access），其处理登录认证所必需的信息。

5. 存储与保留

本应用没有单独的数据库，不另行存储您的信息。登录会话及访问日志由 Cloudflare Access 记录，并按照 Cloudflare 隐私政策 及其日志保留策略进行保存和删除。

6. 撤销授权

您可以随时在 https://myaccount.google.com/permissions 撤销本应用对您 Google 账号的访问权限。

7. 遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策

本应用对从 Google API 获取的信息的使用和传输，将遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的“有限使用”（Limited Use）要求。

8. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请联系：summer19940609@gmail.com

English

This Privacy Policy explains how VeryGoodbye Access ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you sign in with Google. The App is the sign-in gateway for VeryGoodbye's personal self-hosted services, protected by Cloudflare Access.

1. Information we collect

Through Google Sign-In, the App requests only the openid, email, and profile scopes, and receives only your email address and basic profile information such as your name.

2. How we use information

This information is used solely to verify your identity and to determine, through Cloudflare Access, whether you are authorized to access the services. Only the owner's authorized account is granted access.

3. No other Google user data

The App does not access or request any other Google user data (for example Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or Calendar).

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any third parties, except Cloudflare (Cloudflare Access), which acts as the access provider and processes the information necessary for authentication.

5. Storage and retention

The App has no separate database and does not store your information itself. Sign-in sessions and access logs are recorded by Cloudflare Access and retained and deleted in accordance with the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and its log retention policies.

6. Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access to your Google Account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

VeryGoodbye Access's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: summer19940609@gmail.com
