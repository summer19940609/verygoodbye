VeryGoodbye Access

Sign-in gateway for VeryGoodbye's personal self-hosted services · VeryGoodbye 个人自托管服务登录入口

关于本应用

VeryGoodbye Access 是 VeryGoodbye 个人自托管服务的登录入口，由 Cloudflare Access 提供访问保护。用户通过 Google 登录（Sign in with Google）来验证身份；只有所有者本人授权的账号才能访问这些服务，其他账号即使登录成功也会被拒绝访问。

About this app

VeryGoodbye Access is the sign-in gateway for VeryGoodbye's personal self-hosted services, protected by Cloudflare Access. Users sign in with Google to verify their identity. Only the owner's authorized account can access the services; any other account is denied access.
